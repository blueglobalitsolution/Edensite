About Us

Redefining Retirement & Assisted Living

Eden Projects is a specialist property developer with deep expertise in retirement living, assisted care facilities, and complex residential schemes.

We combine entrepreneurial thinking with institutional-grade execution. Our unique team structure enables rapid decision-making while maintaining the highest standards of delivery.

From Grade II Listed buildings to Green Belt developments, we embrace complexity where others see obstacle.

To bridge the gap in the UK housing challenge by aligning planning, funding, and execution to create long-term social and economic value.

We specialise in the delivery of challenging developments that demand expertise, creativity, and resilience.

Established with a clear focus on unlocking complex sites for high-quality urban regeneration across the UK.

Our integrated approach spans acquisition, planning, funding, and delivery — all under one roof.
