THE PEOPLE BEHIND EDEN

Our Team

A unique combination of development expertise, entrepreneurial thinking, and institutional rigour.

Expert Team

Leadership & Delivery Team

An integrated leadership team combining funding, delivery, and commercial expertise — enabling Eden to structure, fund, and deliver complex regeneration and later living schemes with confidence.


“From capital structuring through to construction delivery and operational strategy, Eden’s leadership team provides true end-to-end capability — aligning risk, funding, and execution.”

George Dhillon

Co-Founder & Director

A founding partner of Eden, George brings over 25 years’ experience across civil engineering, construction, and residential development. He leads on strategy, funding, and corporate structuring, with a proven track record of delivering complex schemes exceeding £200m in value.

Hiten Raghwani

Director, Construction & Delivery Lead

Hiten is responsible for construction delivery and project execution, overseeing schemes from planning and pre-construction through to completion. With experience managing construction projects from £10m to £50m, he brings strong capabilities in programme management, contractor coordination, cost control, value engineering and risk mitigation.

Ian Gerrard

Commercial & Financial Control

Ian provides commercial oversight and financial discipline across Eden’s projects. With extensive experience managing developments up to £30m and a background with leading operators including Kier and Churchill Retirement Living, he ensures robust cost control, procurement strategy, and financial reporting. His expertise includes budgeting, funding negotiations, contract management, and stakeholder reporting, supporting the delivery of financially viable and well-governed schemes.
