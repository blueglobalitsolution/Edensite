ACTIVE PORTFOLIO

Current Projects

Active developments with a combined GDV in excess of £250 million, across retirement living, assisted living, and care facilities.

Thorpe, Surrey
Luxury Later Living

The Sandars - Phase 1, 2, & 3

A multi-phase gated village set within 27 acres of Surrey parkland and lakes that blends Grade II listed heritage with contemporary design to provide bespoke 1- and 2-bedroom apartments for residents over 55.

Highlights: Restoration of Eastley End House; social hub at Cullinan House; focus on wellness, community, quality and long-term security.

Wolverhampton
Heritage Preservation & Mixed-Use Regeneration

Beatties

Redevelopment of an iconic former department store preserving the 1877 Art Deco facade and converting the site into a vibrant mixed-use destination with over 300 dwellings and commercial activation.

Highlights: Retention of existing building fabric to minimize carbon impact; creation of public realms including Parkland Walk and Courtyard Gardens; reinstatement of the original arcade to improve urban permeability.

Camden, London, NW5
Assisted Living / Residential

Ingestre Road Residential Assisted Living

A flagship assisted living scheme transforming underutilised urban space into high-quality residential environments that prioritize architectural quality, resident dignity, and community green spaces.

Highlights: Design influenced by local modernist heritage using dark brick and articulated rooflines; integration of Lifetime Homes principles and vibrant community green spaces.

Stanmore
Care Home & Heritage Regeneration

Brockley Hill Care Home & Heritage Regeneration

Sensitive regeneration of a derelict site formerly part of the Royal National Orthopaedic Hospital, balancing preservation of heritage assets with delivery of modern care facilities and enhanced natural landscapes.

Highlights: Preservation of East Gate Lodge and Vine Cottage; project sits over a Scheduled Ancient Monument requiring careful planning; outcome provides modern care environments and community amenities.
