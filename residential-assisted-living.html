Ingestre Road – Residential Assisted Living

Part of a wider regeneration effort in the Camden area, Ingestre Road transforms an urban asset into a high-quality residential environment. The scheme delivers specialized housing designed as an Assisted Living concept to meet real community needs.

The modern architectural language features dark red brick, articulated stepped rooflines, and exposed concrete features that respect the neighbouring modernist and Victorian townscape of Camden.

Sustainability & Landscape Vision

Built to Lifetime Homes principles and compliant with the London Housing Design Guide, the development is fully wheelchair accessible. The landscape vision includes a Community Walk, green street frontage, sunken gardens, and private gardens to enhance the public realm for residents.
