Brockley Hill – Care Home & Heritage Regeneration

Sensitive regeneration of a derelict site formerly part of the Royal National Orthopaedic Hospital, balancing preservation of heritage assets with delivery of modern care facilities and enhanced natural landscapes.

The project respects and retains key architectural features of the original building through sensitive restoration of façades and integration of historic design elements into new structures, providing high-quality residential and healthcare facilities.

Community & Sustainability

The development enhances the local area by combining heritage conservation with modern living needs. A fabric-first strategy minimises embodied carbon through reuse of existing structures and energy-efficient design. The scheme revitalises an unused heritage asset, creates employment opportunities, and provides essential care services to the community.

Property Media

The landscape design features green courtyards, accessible pathways, and outdoor communal spaces designed to improve resident wellbeing and integrate with the surrounding environment. The project sits over a Scheduled Ancient Monument, requiring careful planning to deliver modern care environments alongside preserved community amenities.
